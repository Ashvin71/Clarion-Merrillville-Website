Accessibility

Our aim is to make hotel information and assistance easier to access.

Website accessibility

Clarion Inn Merrillville is committed to providing a website experience that is usable by a broad audience. This site is designed with semantic structure, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus indicators, readable contrast, labeled forms and reduced-motion support.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. If you have trouble accessing information or using a feature on this website, please tell us what you need and how we can reach you.

Property accessibility

Property accessibility features and room configurations are still being confirmed. For specific needs—including room features, routes through the property or service accommodations—please contact the hotel directly before your stay.
