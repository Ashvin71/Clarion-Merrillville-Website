Website Privacy Policy

General information about how this temporary hotel website handles information.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Information you provide

If you contact Clarion Inn Merrillville by email, telephone or the website contact form, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, requested stay dates and message. Please do not submit payment-card information, government identification numbers or other sensitive data through the contact form.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to your inquiry, assist with reservation questions, discuss group needs and provide requested hotel information. Submitting the form does not create or confirm a reservation.

Website operations

This temporary website does not currently describe the use of analytics, advertising cookies or online payment processing. If those services are introduced, this policy should be reviewed and updated to describe them accurately. The hosting provider may process basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the website.

Links to other websites

The site may link to maps, email services, telephone functions or an official booking provider. Those services have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not control them.
