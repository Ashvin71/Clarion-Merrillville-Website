About Our Hotel

A comfortable, convenient home base for time spent in Northwest Indiana.

Clarion Inn Merrillville welcomes business and leisure travelers looking for comfortable lodging and convenient regional access.

Located on Rhode Island Street in Merrillville, the hotel puts guests within easy reach of area shopping, dining, major routes and attractions across Northwest Indiana.

Whether your visit is focused on work, family, an event or a simple overnight stop, our goal is to provide a welcoming place to settle in and a hotel team you can contact directly.
